# Machine Ides

## Plastic Bins
- Have multiple Recycling bins scattered around school that will be filled with different types of plastics
- Plastics Bins will be collected and taken to Recycling center

## Washing 
- Plastic will be dispensed, in loads, in a machine that acts similar to a washing machine

## Sorting
- An Ai camera will be used to identify types of plastics and will properly send each plastic to a designated bin
- Each plastic bin will be used to deposit into the shredder

## Shredding
- Plastic will be deposited into shredder and forced down by a heavy object
- plastic shredded and dropped into a small a bin in the bottom of the shredder
- Bin is removable and can be replaced by an empty one

## Heater
- Plastic will be dumped into heater
- Heater will spin and mix the plastic together
- heater will shoot out plastic and into a container

## Collection coil  
- a coil will be at the end of the heater that spins and collects the 3D print filament
